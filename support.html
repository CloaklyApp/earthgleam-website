frequently asked

keeping a pause

how do I keep a pause?

  1. open earthgleam and let the globe settle on you
  2. tap “keep this pause” once, while location is allowed
  3. the pause settles onto the globe as a small point of light

one a day?

yes — one pause per day, by design. the quiet comes from the limit.

what if I travel somewhere new?

the pause keeps wherever you stand when you tap. the globe follows your sky — the sun, the clouds and the moon all match your own patch of earth.

the sky

are the clouds and the moon real?

yes. the cloud cover is real for the time shown, the sun follows your own sky, and the moon shows the phase it actually has tonight. the globe keeps true time.

what is landscape mode?

turn the phone sideways and the globe rests — a slower view, made for leaving on a table or a wall.

the archive

where does my data live?

in one encrypted file on your phone. it is signed so it cannot be forged or quietly altered, and it never leaves the device on its own.

can I back up or move my pauses?

yes. settings → fragments lets you export your archive as an .egvault file — encrypted and signed — and import it again on any device. monthly backups can be kept in the app's documents folder, visible in the Files app.

what happens if I delete the app?

the archive goes with it. export an .egvault first if you want to keep the year.

badges & the almanac

what are the constellations?

small figures drawn in the sky for the days you kept — streaks of consecutive days, weekends, returns to a place, a full month. they appear on their own; nothing to claim.

when can I see the year's almanac?

any time, place by place. once a year — in the last week of december — the year's plate is pressed and kept, one for each year you used the app.

earthgleam pro

what does pro do?

it quiets the view — strips away numbers, captions, lists and reminders. only the globe and your scattered pauses remain. the globe itself doesn't change.

is it a subscription?

no. one purchase, $3.99. it stays yours.

how do I restore a purchase?

open the purchase screen and tap “restore purchases”. use the same apple id you bought with.

privacy

does earthgleam collect anything?

no. no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. the app contains no networking code at all. see the privacy policy — it is short, because there is little to say.
