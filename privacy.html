privacy

earthgleam collects nothing. your pauses live on your phone, and nowhere else.

what the app stores

each pause you keep is written to a single file inside the app on your device: the day, the coordinate, and the place name. that is the whole archive. there is no account, no cloud copy, and no server that knows it exists.

what leaves the device

nothing, on its own. the app contains no networking code. it does not contact us, any analytics service, any advertising network, or any map provider. only two things can ever take data off the phone, and both require you to act:

  • saving the daily card or the year's plate to photos — the image stays in your library until you share it.
  • exporting your fragments — the file is handed to the iOS share sheet, and you decide where it goes.

location

location is requested only while the app is open, and only to place the current pause on the globe. the coordinate is written to the local archive described above. it is never transmitted.

photos

earthgleam asks for permission to add to your library, never to read it. images are written only when you ask to keep the card or the year's plate.

purchase

the one-time upgrade is handled entirely by apple's app store. we receive no name, email, or payment detail from apple — only, if you contact us, what you choose to write.

analytics and tracking

none. no SDKs, no identifiers, no cookies, no usage reports. there is nothing to opt out of.

children

the app collects no data from anyone, of any age.

your control

deleting the app deletes the archive with it. monthly backups, if you keep them, sit in the app's documents folder and are visible in the files app — yours to move or erase at any time.

contact
